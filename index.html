    How a score gets on the board

    Every AUTO played to 0:00 is recorded, for each alliance running a team's own code. It counts for Top AUTO when every robot on it runs its team's own uploaded code and has a team number, the hive tip rules are the standard ones, the parking zones haven't been moved, and no robot on it crosses onto the other alliance's side (G402, a MAJOR FOUL – warnings such as bumping the hive frame, G417, don't count). Set every robot setting – timings, accuracy, drivetrain, camera, shooter, error – to whatever your robot really does: they never keep a score off the board. Code marked @sim preloads-score and hand-written sim scripts don't count.

    Only the team numbers, the score, a fingerprint of the code and whether it counts are sent – never the code. Each team (or pair of teams) shows its best score. The board updates every few minutes.

          How to use Translate (5 steps)
          1. Look under Needs your answer first – those are calls the simulator can’t work out (your own methods, commands, helpers). Then glance at Check these guesses.
          2. For each call, choose What it does on the field: Drive to a point, Curve to a point, Turn to a heading, Follow one of its Pedro paths, Wait, Spin up the shooter, Launch everything held, Launch some, Intake on / off, Several steps – or Not part of the route.
          3. Fill in Details: a drive takes x y heading (where it ends, heading in degrees), a wait takes seconds, a path takes its name from your code. Use Repeats if the call runs in a loop.
          4. Set Points I type are in to match your numbers: Pedro corner inches (0–144) – origin in the audience-side red corner – or field-center inches (–72 to 72). Heading 0° faces the blue wall, 90° faces the rear wall.
          5. Tick Use my answers, then read What the simulator will run at the bottom and press Play. The button on the card shows Translate ✓ while your answers are in use.
