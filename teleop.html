Practice: drive with the game elements on the field and no clock. Start full match runs a 2:30 match like the real field: AUTO from 2:30 to 2:00 (everyone’s code), an 8-second transition (“Drivers, pick up your controllers… 3, 2, 1”), then TeleOp from 2:00 to 0:00. TeleOp only starts both alliances at 0 points, with the field as it looks after a typical AUTO: the spilled elements on the field and 1 NECTAR per alliance entered (the rest at 1:00, G426).

Pins (G421): pushing an opponent into a wall, the hive frame, a flower or another robot so it can’t move counts as a PIN – a MAJOR FOUL after 3 s, and another every 3 s after that.

Controllers

Plug in your controller – PlayStation, Xbox, REV or Logitech – with USB, or pair it with Bluetooth, then press any button on it. Xbox-style buttons: A = ✕, B = ○, X = □, Y = △. No controller? Choose Keyboard on a robot card.
