How to get on the board

  • AUTO: play an AUTO to 0:00 on the AUTO simulator with your team’s own code and team number. One robot is Solo; two on the same alliance is Duo.
  • TeleOp: on the TeleOp simulator, press Start 2:00 TeleOp with one robot (Solo) or two on the same alliance (Duo), and play to the end.
  • Standard game settings, realistic robot times (flower pull 0.75 s or more, garden pickup 0.5 s or more, launching all 4 in 0.6 s or more, and accuracy left at the standard value), an AprilTag camera chosen (AUTO), and no major fouls (G402). The simulator tells you after each run if it counted.
  • Each team (or pair of teams) shows its best score. The board updates every few minutes. TeleOp scores are on the honor system – please play fair.
